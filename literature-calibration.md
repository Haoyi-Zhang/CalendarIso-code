# Full-paper calibration and closest-work scope

Only complete substantive readings count. Author manuscripts are distinguished
from publisher final typesetting. Bibliographic identification alone does not
count as a full reading or as a novelty determination. No external PDF is packaged.
The original 12/5/5 target remains **1/12 TPDS, 1/5 influential, 2/5 adjacent**;
there is no overlap credit and no claim that this selection is a systematic survey.

| Item and category | Source and reading extent | Pattern and application |
|---|---|---|
| Bertogna, Cirinei, Lipari; TPDS2009; same venue | Author accepted manuscript, 14/14 pages; DOI10.1109/TPDS.2008.129; journal metadata 20(4):553–566 | Nine main sections, 25 references, ten figures. Exact interference condition precedes computable bounds and iterative refinement; figures separate intuition, algorithm and synthetic evidence. Adopt that semantic/algorithmic separation, not its wording or current-venue authority. Its task model is not our reference-gated commitment language. |
| Brandenburg, Gül; RTSS2016; influential | Author manuscript, 12/12 pages; https://people.mpi-sws.org/~bbb/papers/pdf/rtss16b.pdf | Reservation splitting/reclaiming is distinguished from reused mechanisms. Theory, granularity studies and measured implementation costs form separate evidence. The comparison distinguishes inherited theory, composition and model-only measurements from the source paper's physical results. |
| Nasri, Brandenburg; RTSS2017; adjacent | Author manuscript, 12/12 pages; DOI10.1109/RTSS.2017.00009; https://people.mpi-sws.org/~bbb/papers/pdf/rtss17.pdf | Six main sections, 18 references, eight figures; graph construction and exactness precede scalability, merging ablation and timeout limitations. Known finite jobs and independent release/cost intervals differ from our causal all-stream claim. Use explicit theorem premises and report failures, not a generic “exact versus approximate” novelty contrast. |
